# Instructions

## Parts

- Filament
- 2m of 144/m ws2812 led strip
- 18650 battery
- bms for the 18650
- some wire
- xiao esp32-c3


## Assembly

1. Cut up the strip into 8 pieces, and glue them in the channels of the 3d print

    <img src="image.png" alt="Led strip in channel" height="320" />
2. Wire up the strips with small wires

    <img src="image-1.png" alt="Led strip soldered" height="320" />
    <img src="image-2.png" alt="Led strip soldered" height="320" />
3. connect the bms to the 18650 and use capton tape to insulate it
   
   <img src="image-3.png" alt="18650 with bms and capton tape insulation" height="320" />
4. Put everything in the case

    <img src="image-4.png" alt="Everything in the case" height="320" />

5. Solder up the xiao based on the wiring diagram

    <img src="image-5.png" alt="xiao soldered up" height="320" />

6. Snap on the diffusor

    <img src="image-6.png" alt="Final project" height="320" />
